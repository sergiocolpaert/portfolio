## About the project

Complete e-commerce design for CÈFFY, a fruit-based gummy supplement brand. The project covers the shopping journey from storefront to cart, with a light, natural visual identity built to convey care and trust. Designed in Figma and implemented in WordPress with Elementor and PHP, in partnership with <a href="https://www.behance.net/Gabrielribeiroalves" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Gabriel Ribeiro</a>.
